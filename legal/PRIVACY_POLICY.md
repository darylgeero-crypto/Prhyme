## 3. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

### 3.1. Information You Provide Directly

(a) **Account information:** email address, chosen display name, and password (stored as a salted cryptographic hash — we never store plaintext passwords).

(b) **Payment information:** billing name, billing address, and payment method details are processed by our third-party payment processor (Stripe). We receive transaction confirmations and the last four digits of payment cards but do not store full card numbers.

(c) **Content you create or upload:** prompts, parameters, lyrics (including edited versions), beats, mixes, masters, audio uploads, images, and any other creative content you generate or provide through the Service.

(d) **Communications:** messages you send to support, feedback you provide, and survey responses.

### 3.2. Information Collected Automatically

(a) **Usage data:** features accessed, pages visited, buttons pressed, session duration and frequency, genre selections, settings and preferences, search queries within the Service, and interaction patterns.

(b) **Device and technical data:** device model, operating system and version, app version, IP address, browser type, language, timezone, screen resolution, and mobile network information.

(c) **Diagnostic data:** crash reports, error logs, performance metrics, and debugging information.

(d) **Approximate location:** derived from IP address (city/region level). We do not collect precise GPS location unless you explicitly grant permission for a feature requiring it.

### 3.3. Information from Third Parties

(a) If you sign in via a third-party service (Google, Apple, Facebook), we receive the basic profile information you authorize (typically name, email address, and profile identifier).

(b) Our payment processor provides transaction status and limited billing details as described in 3.1(b).

(c) Analytics providers may supply aggregated usage statistics.

### 3.4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

(a) We use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies to: keep you signed in, remember preferences (e.g., explicit-content toggle, ambient mix setting, theme), measure usage, and support Service functionality.

(b) Our mobile and desktop applications use local storage equivalents for the same purposes.

(c) You may disable cookies in your browser, but some features may not function correctly.

## 4. HOW WE USE INFORMATION

4.1. We use personal information for the following purposes:

(a) **Providing the Service:** creating and managing your Account, delivering features, processing your creative requests, and rendering AI-generated output.

(b) **Billing:** processing payments, managing subscriptions, issuing refunds, and preventing fraud.

(c) **Improvement and development:** training, fine-tuning, and evaluating generative models; developing new features; conducting research; performing analytics. Where feasible, we use de-identified or aggregated data for these purposes.

(d) **Personalization:** remembering your preferences, recommending genres or features, and tailoring your experience.

(e) **Communications:** sending transactional messages (receipts, password resets, trial expiry notices), responding to support requests, and — with your consent where required — sending marketing communications.

(f) **Safety and enforcement:** detecting abuse, enforcing the Terms of Service, investigating violations, and protecting the rights and safety of Prhyme and users.

(g) **Legal compliance:** complying with legal obligations, responding to lawful requests, and establishing or defending legal claims.

4.2. **Legal basis / consent.** Under PIPEDA, we rely on your consent (express or implied depending on the sensitivity and context), as well as the necessity of processing to provide the Service you requested. You may withdraw consent subject to legal and contractual restrictions, as described in Section 9.

## 5. HOW WE SHARE INFORMATION

5.1. **We do not sell your personal information.** We share personal information only as described below:

(a) **Service providers:** payment processors (Stripe), cloud hosting providers, email delivery services, crash-reporting and analytics providers, and customer-support tools — each bound by contractual obligations to protect your information and use it only for the services they provide to us.

(b) **AI and infrastructure partners:** where generative features rely on third-party APIs, the minimum content necessary to fulfil your request may be transmitted to those providers under their data-processing terms.

(c) **Legal and safety:** we may disclose information if required by law, court order, or governmental request; to enforce our Terms; to detect fraud or security issues; or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Prhyme, users, or the public.

(d) **Business transfers:** in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, personal information may be transferred to the successor entity, subject to equivalent protections.

(e) **With your consent:** we share information for other purposes with your explicit consent.

5.2. **Aggregated and de-identified data.** We may share anonymized, aggregated, or de-identified data that cannot reasonably identify you for research, analytics, or product development without restriction.

## 6. DATA RETENTION

6.1. We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this Policy, including:

(a) **Account data:** retained while your Account is active and for up to 24 months after closure, unless longer retention is required by law (e.g., tax records).

(b) **Content data:** AI-Generated Content and User-Generated Content associated with your Account is retained while your Account is active. Upon Account deletion, we will delete or anonymize content within 90 days, except: (i) Purchased Content records retained for transaction history; (ii) content incorporated into anonymized training datasets, which cannot be reverse-identified; and (iii) content we are legally required to retain.

(c) **Payment records:** retained for a minimum of 7 years to comply with tax and financial regulations.

(d) **Logs and diagnostics:** typically retained for 12 months, then deleted or aggregated.

6.2. You may request earlier deletion as described in Section 9, subject to the exceptions above.

## 7. DATA SECURITY

7.1. We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards, including:

(a) Encryption of data in transit (TLS) and encryption of sensitive data at rest.

(b) Passwords stored using PBKDF2-SHA256 with per-user salts (260,000 iterations).

(c) Access controls limiting employee and contractor access to personal information on a need-to-know basis.

(d) Regular security reviews of our systems.

7.2. **No method is 100% secure.** While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your Account credentials.

7.3. In the event of a data breach involving a real risk of significant harm, we will notify affected individuals and report to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada as required by PIPEDA.

## 8. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

8.1. The Service is operated from Canada. However, our service providers (including payment processing, cloud hosting, and AI infrastructure) may store or process data in other countries, including the United States.

8.2. Where data is transferred outside Canada, it may be subject to the laws of that jurisdiction. We use contractual protections and select providers with appropriate security practices.
