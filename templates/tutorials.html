Tutorials

Short guides for everything Prhyme™ does — a few related tools per card, step by step.

Prhyming

Create

♫

Make a Beat

  1. Genre sets the drum feel — Drill, Trap, BoomBap, Jersey Club, Afrobeats and more. Artist shapes it like a specific producer's style instead.
  2. BPM is the tempo; Length picks a 16/32-bar loop or a full 2:30–3:30 song with intro, verses, hooks and outro.
  3. Kit picks your drum sounds. Hit Generate, preview it right on the page.
  4. Fresh beats land in Recent Generations below — play the last 5 anytime. Hit Download on the one you love to save it to your library; the rest rotate out as you make more.

Try it →

🎚

Shape Your Beat

  1. Melody picks the lead instrument (pluck, keys, flute, choir pads and more). Era flavours the whole beat — 90s, 2000s, modern.
  2. Fills, hat rolls, risers/builds, crash accents and intro/outro add arrangement movement so loops don't feel static.
  3. Swing loosens the groove, humanize adds tiny timing/velocity imperfections, 808 glide slides your bass notes.
  4. Fast mode renders lighter and quicker — handy on low battery. 🕉 Healing swaps drums for Fibonacci melodies, 432 Hz tuning and binaural layers.

Write Lyrics

  1. Theme sets the subject — street, hustle, faith, love, victory and more.
  2. Artist style borrows a flow, vocabulary and cadence — you always get an original draft "in the style of," never their actual words.
  3. Generate a full song: verses, hook and bridge with phonetic rhyme detection keeping the rhymes fresh.
  4. Send any draft to Freestyle to perform it on the teleprompter.

🎛

Kits, Samples & Presets

  1. Soundkits — 6 built-in drum kits (Classic, Punchy, Boomy, Street, Smooth, Hard). Preview every drum before you commit.
  2. Upload your own WAV drums to build a custom kit, then select it on the Beats page.
  3. Samples — upload, tag and preview your own sounds and one-shots.
  4. Presets — one-tap starting points: curated beat and mastering chains when you don't want to start from zero.

Produce

◉

Master a Track

  1. Upload your vocal or full track (WAV, MP3 or M4A) and name it.
  2. Fully Automated Mode (on by default) detects genre, BPM and settings for you — just hit Master. Turn it off for all 32 manual controls.
  3. Keep original beat is on by default so nothing gets generated behind your back — uncheck it to have a beat built for the track.
  4. You get a balanced master at streaming loudness with a full report (LUFS, true peak, BPM, FX used).

Try it →

🎛

Mastering Controls, Explained

  1. Vocal chain — vocal polish (noise cleanup + clarity), de-esser (tames harsh S sounds), timing fix (nudges off-beat words onto the grid).
  2. Creative FX — radio, vinyl and TV textures, high/low-pass filters, and BPM-synced echo with mix, feedback and timing controls.
  3. Mastering — chain preset, loudness target (LUFS), ceiling (dB), multiband compression, dither, fade in/out, and output format (MP3, WAV, FLAC).
  4. A/B compare (on by default) renders 30-second before/after previews so you can hear exactly what the master did.

🎚

Studio (DAW)

  1. Stack beats, vocals and samples across 8 timeline tracks. Drag clips to move them, drag edges to trim.
  2. Mix with per-track volume, mute/solo and the master fader.
  3. Hit record to lay vocals straight into the timeline over your beat.
  4. Bounce the whole session to a finished mixdown file.

Try it →

🎧

Stems: Separate & Master

  1. Separate — upload any track and split it into Vocals, Instrumental, plus an auto-generated SFX layer (risers, impacts and whooshes placed at section changes).
  2. Download each layer on its own for remixes and edits.
  3. Stem mastering — upload 2–8 stems (vocals, drums, bass, synths…) and the Master page balances them into one polished stereo mix.

Perform

🎧

Live DJ

  1. Load tracks into Deck A and Deck B — your generated beats or your own uploads.
  2. Blend with the crossfader, tempo sliders and 3-band EQ. SYNC beatmatches for you; set cue points to jump around a track.
  3. Or hand it to auto-mix: it lines up the next track by BPM and blends with a crossfade transition.

🎤

Freestyle Assister

  1. The teleprompter highlights your lyrics word-by-word on the beat while you perform.
  2. Generate a full draft verse, or get phrase sparks — fresh lines fed to you every few bars.
  3. Rhyme-word mode flashes one rhyme word per bar to keep your flow going when you're stuck.
  4. Pick any beat to freestyle over, or use the built-in click track.

Library

♫

Player, Files & Tools

  1. Player — your generated tracks always list first, then Music, then Downloads. The Now Playing card gives you big artwork and controls; Search Online finds any song on Spotify, YouTube and SoundCloud.
  2. Files — every generated track, upload and temp file with inline players, downloads and deletes. History keeps every render re-downloadable.
  3. Tools — trim audio, normalize loudness, karaoke (vocal cut), make ringtones, detect key. Convert turns any audio file into WAV, MP3, OGG, FLAC or M4A.
