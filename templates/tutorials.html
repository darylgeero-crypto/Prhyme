Tutorials

Quick video-style guides for everything Prhyme™ does. Each card walks you through a feature step by step.

Prhyming

♫

Make a Beat

  1. Pick a genre — Drill, Trap, BoomBap, Jersey Club, Afrobeats and more.
  2. Or pick an artist — the engine matches their production style.
  3. Set BPM and bars, choose a drum kit and lead instrument.
  4. Hit Generate — your beat renders in about a second. Preview it right on the page.

Try it →

◉

Master a Track

  1. Upload your vocal recording (WAV, MP3 or M4A).
  2. Vocal polish cleans noise and adds clarity — timing fix nudges off-beat words onto the grid.
  3. Add creative FX — radio, vinyl, TV, filters, BPM-synced echo.
  4. Get a balanced mix mastered to -14 LUFS / -1.0 dBTP.

Try it →

✎

Draft Lyrics

  1. Pick a theme — street, hustle, faith, love, victory and more.
  2. Pick an artist style for flow, vocabulary and cadence.
  3. Generate a full song with verses, hook and bridge.
  4. Send it to Freestyle to perform it on the teleprompter.

Try it →

🎧

Live DJ

  1. Load beats into Deck A and Deck B, or upload your own MP3s.
  2. Use the crossfader, tempo sliders and 3-band EQ to blend.
  3. Hit SYNC to match tempos, set cue points to jump around.
  4. Or let Rockdabus Prhyme take over — generate a full beatmatched mix by genre or era.

Try it →

🎤

Freestyle Assister

  1. Teleprompter highlights lyrics word-by-word on the beat.
  2. Generate a full draft verse, or get phrase sparks every few bars.
  3. Rhyme-word mode flashes one word per bar to keep your flow.
  4. Pick a beat to freestyle over, or use the click track.

Try it →

Studio (DAW)

  1. Stack beats, vocals and samples on 8 timeline tracks.
  2. Drag clips to move them, drag edges to trim.
  3. Mix with per-track volume, mute/solo and the master fader.
  4. Hit the record button to lay vocals straight in, then bounce your mixdown.

🤖 Fully Automated Mastering

The Master page now has Fully Automated Mode (ON by default). Just upload your track and hit "Master it" — Prhyme detects genre, BPM, and optimal settings automatically.

Uncheck the box to access all 32 manual controls: mastering chain presets, LUFS targets, multiband, de-essing, vocal FX (radio/TV/vinyl), echo, filters, fades, and stem export.

🎧 Smart DJ Selection

The DJ's auto-mix now uses intelligent track selection. Instead of just playing the next track, it picks the best transition based on:

  • BPM matching — within 6% for smooth beatmatching
  • Harmonic mixing — Camelot wheel key compatibility
  • Energy curve — builds energy gradually, avoids crashes

Use the /api/dj/smart_next endpoint or enable auto-mix on the DJ page.

🎙️ Rokdabus Upgrades

Rokdabus Prhyme has three new enhancements:

  • Smarter responses — 10 producer wisdom tips + genre-specific advice
  • Proactive DJ — Hype phrases ("Let's go! This one's fire!") and transition announcements
  • Voice upgrade — Prefers Google US English voice, natural pacing with pauses

🎵 Player Now Playing

The Music Player now has a Now Playing card with large album art (200px), track title, and playback controls — Spotify-style. It appears when you start playing a track.

✍️ Lyric Engine v2

The lyric generator now uses phonetic rhyme detection (134k word dictionary) instead of hardcoded rhyme keys. It ensures:

  • Rhyme diversity > 0.8 (no more "same rhymes")
  • 15 candidate lines ranked per position
  • Temperature + repetition penalties for variety

🥁 Beat Humanization

Beats now have human feel:

  • Per-instrument timing bias + Gaussian jitter (±10ms)
  • Velocity variation for dynamics
  • Round-robin drum samples (no machine-gun effect)
  • Genre-specific swing presets

🌐 Online Music Search

The Player now has Search Online. Type a song name and get instant links to:

  • 🎵 Spotify — Full catalog search, opens in Spotify app
  • 📺 YouTube — Music videos and live performances
  • ☁️ SoundCloud — Independent artists and remixes

Find it in the Player page under the Now Playing card.

🎚️ Stem Mastering (2-8 Stems)

Upload 2-8 separate stems (vocals, drums, bass, synths, etc.) and master them together as a complete mix. Found on the Master page.

The backend balances levels, applies your mastering chain, and exports a polished stereo master.
