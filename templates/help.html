♫ Making Beats

  1. Pick a genre — Drill, Trap, BoomBap, Jersey Club, Afrobeats and more.
  2. Or pick an artist — the engine emulates their production style (drums, 808s, melodies). Style only — never copies real beats.
  3. Set BPM & bars — tempo and length (8 / 16 / 32 bars).
  4. Pick a drum kit & lead — 6 built-in kits (Classic, Punchy, Boomy…) plus any custom kits you built, and 10 lead instruments from pluck to choir.
  5. Generate — renders in about a second. Hit play right on the page.
  6. 🕉 Healing mode (optional) — Fibonacci-ratio melodies, 432/440 Hz tuning, and a drone bed that cycles solfeggio tones, the seven chakras, or planetary frequencies. Binaural beat bands (delta → gamma) for headphone listening, plus presets: Deep Healing, Energy Cleanse, Meditation, Chakra Journey, Deep Sleep. Wellness-vibe tones for relaxation and focus — no health claims.
💡 Start with 16 bars at the artist's suggested BPM — the app fills it in automatically when you pick an artist. Upload your own WAV drums under Soundkits for a fully personal kit.

◉ Mastering a Track

  1. Upload your recording — WAV, MP3 or M4A of your vocals.
  2. Vocal polish (on by default) — cleans noise, adds clarity, tames harshness. Never deletes or cuts words.
  3. Timing fix (on by default) — nudges slightly off-beat words onto the grid. Max ~50ms, keeps your natural feel.
  4. Creative FX (optional) — 📻 radio, 📺 TV, 💿 old vinyl, high/low-pass filters, 🔊 echo (can sync to your BPM). Tap a preset chip like Lo-Fi or Radio Hit, or dial it in yourself. Apply to the vocal or the full mix.
  5. Get a beat + mix + master — balanced mix, mastered to -14 LUFS / -1.0 dBTP.
💡 If your take is already on beat, timing fix does nothing — it's a safety net, not an effect.

🎧 DJ Mode

  1. Load tracks — tap 📀 to pull from your generated beats, or upload your own MP3s.
  2. Two decks — play/pause, seek on the waveform, set cue points.
  3. Mix live — crossfader, tempo sliders, 3-band EQ per deck. Hit SYNC to match tempos.
  4. Generate a Mix — pick a genre or hit-era style (2000s Southern Trap, 2010s Drill, 2020s Melodic) and get a beatmatched continuous mix.
💡 Filenames with BPM (like …-140bpm-…) auto-fill the deck tempo.

🎤 Freestyle Assister

  1. Teleprompter — lyrics highlight word-by-word on the beat. Set BPM, press play, ride it.
  2. Full verse — generate a draft verse on a theme, send it to the teleprompter.
  3. Phrase sparks — a short original phrase every 2 / 4 / 8 bars to spark your next line.
  4. Rhyme word — one rhyming word flashes on the bar to keep your flow going.
💡 Pick a beat from the dropdown to freestyle over, or use the built-in click track.
